# TechCorp SaaS — Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** TechCorp Solutions Inc.
**Product:** TechCorp Platform — A cloud-based business operations platform
**Founded:** 2019
**Headquarters:** San Francisco, CA
**Employees:** 250
**Customers:** 5,000+ businesses across 40 countries
**Annual Revenue:** $25M ARR

## Product Suite

### 1. TechCorp Core (Flagship Product)
A unified workspace for teams to manage projects, track tasks, collaborate in real-time, and automate workflows.

**Key Features:**
- Project Management: Kanban boards, Gantt charts, timeline views
- Task Management: Assign, track, and prioritize tasks
- Team Collaboration: Comments, file sharing, @mentions
- Workflow Automation: Triggers, rules, and integrations
- Reporting & Analytics: Dashboards, custom reports, KPIs
- API Access: RESTful API for custom integrations

**Pricing Tiers:**
- **Starter (Free):** Up to 5 users, 1GB storage, basic features
- **Professional ($29/user/month):** Unlimited users, 50GB storage, automation, API
- **Enterprise ($79/user/month):** Unlimited everything, SSO, priority support, custom integrations, SLA

### 2. TechCorp Connect
Integration hub connecting TechCorp Core with 200+ third-party tools (Slack, GitHub, Jira, Salesforce, etc.)

### 3. TechCorp Analytics
Advanced analytics and business intelligence module with custom dashboards and predictive insights.

## Support Structure

**Current Support Team:**
- 8 human agents (covering US, EU, APAC time zones)
- 2 senior escalation engineers
- 1 support manager
- Average response time: 4 hours (email), 30 minutes (chat)
- Support hours: 24/5 (no weekends)

**Pain Points:**
- 60% of tickets are repetitive questions (password reset, how-to, billing)
- Weekend coverage gaps cause 48+ hour delays
- After-hours inquiries pile up, creating Monday backlog
- Agent burnout from repetitive tasks
- Inconsistent response quality across agents
- No unified customer view across channels

## Brand Values

- **Customer Obsession:** Every interaction should make the customer feel valued
- **Speed:** Fast responses matter more than perfect responses
- **Accuracy:** Never give wrong information — better to say "I'll find out" than guess
- **Empathy:** Understand the customer's frustration before jumping to solutions
- **Proactivity:** Anticipate needs and offer help before being asked
